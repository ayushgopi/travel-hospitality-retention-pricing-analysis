# Week 4 Executive Summary

The final analysis confirms that cancellation risk is a major revenue-management problem: the cleaned dataset contains 86,638 bookings with a 27.69% cancellation rate. Average ADR is 107.04, average lead time is 80.0 days, and Summer contributes the highest gross booking value. The Week 3 baseline model selected Logistic Regression as the strongest model by ROC-AUC (0.854), making it suitable as a starting point for cancellation-risk scoring and retention prioritization.
